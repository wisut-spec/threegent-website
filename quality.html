Our Process

We Supply to Grade — Not Guesswork

All minerals we supply are analyzed by accredited third-party laboratories and documented with full Certificates of Analysis (COA). Buyers receive complete compositional data — including oxide percentages and key physical parameters — before finalizing any order.

01

Accredited Lab Analysis

Every product lot is tested at an accredited third-party laboratory using standard analytical methods (including WDXRF for elemental composition). Test results are fully documented and traceable to each shipment.

02

Certificate of Analysis on Every Order

A full Certificate of Analysis is issued with every shipment. For gypsum, this covers 15 parameters. For dolomite and kaolin, WDXRF elemental composition is provided. Buyers can request the COA at inquiry stage — before placing any order.

03

Transparent Pricing from First Inquiry

No vague market-rate quotes. We provide itemised FOB pricing — product, packaging (Bigbag), and logistics — so procurement teams can build landed-cost models with real numbers from the first conversation.
