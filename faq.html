Products & Specifications

Gypsum is a naturally occurring calcium sulfate mineral (CaSO₄·2H₂O) widely used across construction, agriculture, and industrial manufacturing.

Three Gent supplies high-purity gypsum from Thailand with the following typical specification:

  • Purity (CaSO₄·2H₂O): 95.89%
  • Whiteness: 87.80
  • pH: 8.50

Common applications include cement retarder, plasterboard, soil amendment, and industrial filler.

Typical specification. Exact composition may vary by lot. Full COA available upon request.

Dolomite is a calcium magnesium carbonate mineral (CaMg(CO₃)₂) used in steelmaking, construction, agriculture, and glass manufacturing.

Three Gent supplies dolomite from Thailand with the following typical specification:

  • CaO: 34.196%
  • MgO: 21.638%
  • SiO₂: 0.360%
  • Al₂O₃: 0.384%
  • Fe₂O₃: 0.126%

Typical specification. Exact composition may vary by lot. Full COA available upon request.

Kaolin is a fine white clay mineral used in ceramics, paper coating, paint, rubber, and plastics manufacturing.

Three Gent supplies kaolin from Thailand with the following typical specification:

  • SiO₂: 22.966%
  • Al₂O₃: 5.681%
  • CaO: 24.435%
  • MgO: 5.878%
  • Fe₂O₃: 1.237%
  • K₂O: 2.316%
  • TiO₂: 0.283%

Typical specification. Exact composition may vary by lot. Full COA available upon request.

Three Gent currently supplies three industrial minerals for export:

  • Gypsum — construction, cement, agriculture
  • Dolomite — steelmaking, construction, glass
  • Kaolin — ceramics, paper, paint, plastics

All products are sourced from verified Thai suppliers, lab-tested, and available with full COA documentation.

Ordering & Shipping

Yes. Three Gent exports to buyers across Southeast Asia and East Asia, including Japan, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia, and the Philippines. All shipments are arranged on FOB (Free On Board) terms from Thai ports.

Contact us for freight routing, estimated transit times, and port options for your destination.

Our standard MOQ is 1 FCL (Full Container Load), approximately 25 MT, depending on product and container size.

Larger volumes are available and may qualify for improved pricing. Contact us to discuss your requirement.

Standard payment terms are T/T — 30% deposit upon order confirmation, 70% payable upon Bill of Lading (B/L).

Letter of Credit (L/C) is accepted for qualified buyers upon request. Contact us to discuss the arrangement that works for your organisation.

Sample Requests

Yes. Three Gent provides mineral samples to qualified buyers prior to bulk order commitment.

Sample quantity is typically 1–2 kg per product. Samples are provided at no charge. Shipping cost is charged at cost and will be confirmed before dispatch.

To request a sample, contact us via the enquiry form or WhatsApp with your company name, destination, and product of interest.

To process your sample request, please provide:

  • Company name and country
  • Product of interest (Gypsum / Dolomite / Kaolin)
  • Intended application or industry
  • Shipping address

We will confirm availability and dispatch timeline within 1 business day.
